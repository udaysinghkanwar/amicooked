What you can ask

Plain questions, in Claude or ChatGPT. It looks things up in your actual courses before it answers.

  • How bad is next week looking?

    Warming up. Quiet until Thursday, then two labs land back to back. Start one early and it’s fine.

    am_i_cooked

  • What’s my average in stats right now?

    78.4% across the 55% of the course that’s been graded. The midterm is carrying you; the quizzes aren’t.

    get_my_grades

  • What’s due before Sunday?

    Three things: lab report 4 on Thursday, the essay draft Friday at noon, quiz 7 on Sunday night.

    get_assignments

  • Grab the week 9 slides and boil them down.

    Downloaded Week9-Entropy.pdf. Six ideas, and the two your prof kept repeating.

    download_file

  • Did my prof say anything about the midterm?

    Yesterday: it moved to the gym, calculators are allowed, chapters 4 to 7.

    get_announcements

  • Where’s the lab manual?

    Content › Labs › Lab manual, a 2.1 MB PDF. Want the safety section pulled out?

    get_course_content

How it works

Your AI asks Am I Cooked. Am I Cooked asks your school, logged in as you. Nobody else is in the middle.

  1. Youask a question
  2. chat
  3. Claude or ChatGPTdecides what to look up
  4. MCP
  5. on your laptopamicookedfetches it, logged in as you
  6. your session
  7. Your school’s D2Lgrades, content, news
Runs on your laptop
It’s a small app you install, not a website you hand your account to.
Your password never touches us
You log in to your school in a real browser window, like you always do. Am I Cooked keeps the session, never the password.
ChatGPT gets a private tunnel
ChatGPT lives in the cloud, so setup opens a private tunnel to your laptop, locked with a sign-in only you can approve.
Open source, MIT
Read every line on GitHub. Fork it, fix it, send a PR.

Questions

Does it work at my school?

If your school uses D2L Brightspace, probably. Setup has presets for Ontario schools and accepts any Brightspace address. It’s tested at McMaster; if yours misbehaves, open an issue.

Is this an official D2L or university thing?

No. Am I Cooked isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by D2L or any university. Brightspace is a trademark of D2L Corporation.

Will it do my assignments or submit for me?

No. It reads your courses so your AI can answer questions about them. It doesn’t submit work.

What do I need for ChatGPT?

Developer mode turned on in ChatGPT, so it can add a custom connector. Your laptop also has to be awake, because that’s where Am I Cooked runs. Claude Desktop and Claude Code connect to it on your laptop directly.

Where do my files live?

In ~/.amicooked on your laptop: your school session, settings and anything you download.

How do I get rid of it?

Run amicooked uninstall to remove it from your laptop.
