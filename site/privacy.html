Am I Cooked

Privacy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

Who we are

Am I Cooked is a free, open-source app run by its maintainers (see LICENSE). Contact us through GitHub issues.

Your courses stay on your computer

Am I Cooked runs on your computer. Your Brightspace sign-in, courses, grades and files go between your computer, your school and the AI app you chose. They never reach our servers.

Anonymous counts

Setup and updates send anonymous counts: which preset school (or "custom"), your platform, and version numbers. No IP address, install ID or anything from Brightspace is stored. Set DO_NOT_TRACK=1 or AMICOOKED_NO_TELEMETRY=1 to turn this off.

Update emails (optional)

At the end of amicooked setup you can enter an email to get occasional product-update emails, such as new features and fixes. Typing N/A continues without joining the email list. Blank or invalid entries are re-prompted. Students on Ontario K-12 school-board Brightspace are never asked.

When you choose N/A, we record a separate anonymous signup event: the time, app version and source (CLI setup). A random submission ID prevents retries from being counted twice; it is not a persistent device or installation ID. No email address or email consent is stored for this event. This count measures anonymous submissions, not unique people. DO_NOT_TRACK=1 or AMICOOKED_NO_TELEMETRY=1 disables it. Cancelling the prompt records no signup.

  • What we keep: your email, when you signed up, the wording you agreed to, and the app version. Nothing else.
  • Why: only to send Am I Cooked updates. We never sell or share it, and never use it for ads.
  • Where: stored with Upstash (Redis) through Vercel, in the United States, where it may be accessible to US authorities under US law. When we send email we will use an email service, also likely in the US.
  • Unsubscribe: every email has an unsubscribe link, honoured within 10 business days. You can also ask us to delete your email at any time through the contact above.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After an unsubscribe we keep only what we need to never email you again.
  • Age: the list is for post-secondary students. If someone under 18 signed up, contact us and we will delete it.

Questions or complaints

Contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.
