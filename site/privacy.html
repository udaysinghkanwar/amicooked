Privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026.

Your course connection runs on your computer. We do not collect your school password or store your course content on our application servers. Optional update emails and limited usage counts are separate, as explained below.

Your computer, school and AI app

Am I Cooked is a free, open-source project maintained by Uday Kanwar, Sachin Gupta and Akshay Gupta. Its local server reads your Brightspace courses and makes the information you request available to the AI app you connect. Course access is read-only: it does not submit assignments or change your grades.

You sign in on your school’s own page in your browser. Am I Cooked saves session tokens and cookies locally so it can stay connected; it does not ask for or save your school password. Settings, session data, caches and logs are normally stored under ~/.amicooked. Downloaded course files and generated lessons are saved in your configured local files folder.

Local software does not mean offline AI. Course information returned to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another connected service may be processed and retained by that provider under its policies and your account settings. If you enable a remote connection, requests can pass through Cloudflare tunnel infrastructure; our provisioning service stores tunnel identifiers, creation/last-active times and connection-management records. Those records are separate from course content.

Optional emails about updates

If you enter your email at the optional setup prompt, we may email you about Am I Cooked releases, new features, fixes and other product updates. An email address is not required to install or use Am I Cooked. Enter N/A or NA, in any letter case, to continue without joining the email list.

We store your normalized email address, signup time, setup source, app version and the identifier for the prompt you were shown. We use the address for Am I Cooked updates, not advertising for other products, and do not sell it. Service providers may process it to host the list or deliver messages.

You can withdraw from update emails or request access, correction or deletion using the private contact options below. Any product-update email we send must identify the sender, include contact information and offer a working, free way to unsubscribe. Requests to stop update emails will be honoured within 10 business days. Signup storage exists today; this app does not currently include an email-delivery or automated unsubscribe service.

Anonymous counts and choosing N/A

The app sends limited setup and successful-upgrade counts: a preset school identifier (or “custom”), OS/architecture, version numbers, update mode and native/npm channel where relevant. These counters contain no course data, email address or persistent installation identifier.

Choosing N/A can record a separate anonymous signup event containing its time, setup source, app version and a random submission ID. The ID prevents retries from counting twice; it is not an email signup or a persistent device ID. Cancelling the email prompt records no signup. These events measure submissions, not unique people.

Set AMICOOKED_NO_TELEMETRY=1 or DO_NOT_TRACK=1 in the environment running the app to disable app telemetry and anonymous N/A events. This does not cancel an email subscription you separately choose or disable your AI provider’s processing.

Website and service providers

The landing page loads Vercel Web Analytics and records aggregate install-command copy and setup-prompt-open counts. These website counts are separate from the app’s telemetry settings. We do not set advertising cookies in our own website code.

Vercel hosts the website and signup API; Upstash Redis stores signup records and counters. Requests also expose normal network information, including an IP address, to hosting providers. The signup endpoint uses a salted hash of the date and IP address for abuse prevention; that rate-limit key expires after 24 hours. It does not save the raw IP address in the signup record. Providers may maintain their own operational or security logs.

Installers and updates download software from services such as GitHub and npm. AI services, school systems and any enabled tunnel providers have their own privacy policies. Providers may process information outside Canada, depending on their infrastructure and configuration, where different laws may apply.

Retention and removal

Email and anonymous signup records do not currently expire automatically. We retain email records for update subscriptions and consent records, and handle removal requests through the maintainers. After withdrawal, we may retain only information needed to honour the opt-out or meet a legal obligation. Aggregate counters are not linked to an identified person; we generally cannot locate an anonymous event from your name or email.

Disconnecting the app does not by itself delete all local data. The uninstall command’s --purge option removes stored login/settings data; downloaded files, generated lessons, AI chat history and provider-held records may need to be deleted separately. Review your configured folders before deleting files.

Young users

The optional email list is intended for post-secondary students aged 18 or older. Setup skips the email prompt for recognized Ontario K–12 Brightspace domains, but it does not verify every user’s age. If an under-18 email address has been submitted, contact us privately so we can remove it.

Contact and your choices

For private privacy requests, message a maintainer: Uday Kanwar, Sachin Gupta or Akshay Gupta. You can also use GitHub issues to request a private contact channel. Do not post your email address, school credentials, tokens or course information in a public issue.

We may ask for enough information to verify a request before changing an email record. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or Quebec’s Commission d’accès à l’information. We will update this page when our practices change; a new purpose for using your email will require an appropriate new choice.
