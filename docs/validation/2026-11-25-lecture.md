The artifacts, JSON test receipts and editable lessons are retained locally under `Validation 2026-11-25`. No private course materials were used or published. Rendering requires media tools that normal installation does not install.

## Limits

Direct human listening and a continuous human watch-through remain acceptance checks. Transcription and frame review do not substitute for those. Windows/Linux media exports and generation inside other AI hosts were not executed here. Dependency audit reports the existing Zod 3 advisory GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5; resolving its major-version migration is separate from this feature. Renderer organizational warnings remain recorded in each generated check report.
